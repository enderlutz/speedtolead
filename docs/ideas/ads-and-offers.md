## Unlimited touch-ups, monthly (Alan, 2026-10-28)
A monthly plan for customers who always want the fence looking new: they pay a set amount each month and can call us for touch-ups whenever they want (scrapes, a faded run, a swapped board stained to match). Talk through later: price per month (likely tied to fence size), what counts as a touch-up vs a full restain, how many visits, minimum term, whether a full restain at the end of the package's life is included or discounted. Recurring revenue is what investors like.
