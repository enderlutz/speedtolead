  As soon as possible / I'm flexible / Specific dates. Website form first; the ads can wait.
- **Let the customer pick the date they want** (2026-10-20). A lead who names a
  day is a serious one, and we know it before we call.
- **Promise the callback time on the form** (2026-10-20), once On call can keep it:
  "Fill this out any time between [start] and 8 PM and we call you back in under
  5 minutes." The window comes from the lead-arrival data (see on-call.md).
