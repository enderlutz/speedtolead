# Speed to Lead: the 5-Minute System

Sterling Fence Staining · October 19, 2026 · For Alan and Cruz

## The goal
- Every new lead gets a phone call within 5 minutes, 8 AM to 8 PM, every day.
- Somebody owns every minute of that window. No gaps, no excuses.
- Like a drive-thru timer: the clock starts when the customer fills out the form, and every lead is scored.
- We get about 10 leads a day. Leads called in under 5 minutes close at about 15%; leads never called close at 3.5%. Calling every lead fast is worth an estimated $7,000 to $14,000 more profit a month.

## What happens when a lead comes in (live now)
1. The customer fills out the Facebook form. GoHighLevel sends them the automatic intro text.
2. The dashboard picks up the lead and starts the 5-minute clock from the moment the form was filled out, not from when we noticed it.
3. The lead goes to whoever is on the on-call schedule right then. If two people are on at once, they take turns.
4. The lead is assigned to that person in GoHighLevel, so the GoHighLevel app on their iPhone notifies them. They call from that app.
5. They open the customer's profile on the dashboard (MacBook) and measure while they talk.
6. Everybody (Alan, Olga, Fragne) still gets the usual copy of every new lead.
7. The clock stops on its own when GoHighLevel logs their call to that customer. They can also tap "I called" on the dashboard.
8. At 5 minutes with no call, Alan gets a text with the customer's name, phone, and who was on call. Every time, no exceptions.
9. After 8 PM nobody is woken up. Those leads wait, and at 8 AM they all go to whoever is on first, as one list, and the clock starts at 8 AM.
10. A repeat form fill from an old lead counts as a brand-new lead and starts a fresh clock.

## Where it lives in the dashboard: "On call" in the menu
- Now (everyone on the office team): who is on call, and every lead waiting for a first call with a live clock that turns yellow at 3 minutes and red at 5. Buttons: Call, Profile, I called.
- Schedule (everyone sees it, only Alan edits): the week, day by day, who is on and when. "Copy last week" fills a new week in one tap. Any hour from 8 AM to 8 PM with nobody on call shows in red as a gap.
- Scorecard (admin only): per person, % of leads called within 5 minutes, average minutes to the first call, missed leads, and close rate.
- Report (admin only): every lead with its minutes to the first call and a flag: On time (5 minutes or less), Late (over 5), Missed (no call within an hour), Waiting. A "flags per person" line shows who missed. "Only flagged" filters to the problems.
- Team (admin only): for each person, their phone, how they get alerts (WhatsApp or text), their GoHighLevel login, and a "Send a test" button.

## The rules for whoever is on call
- When you are on the schedule, you are on the clock. Lunch or no lunch is your business; the 5 minutes are not optional.
- Hand-offs happen on the schedule, not by message. If your block ends at 2 PM, the 2:01 PM lead belongs to the next person.
- Call first, then send the estimate (or send it after a no-answer). Follow the First Call script.
- No answer: call again and leave a voicemail. By 6 PM call once more and send the estimate anyway.

## How to turn WhatsApp on (one-time setup, about 1 to 3 days including Meta's approval)
1. In GoHighLevel, open the Sterling location, then Settings, then WhatsApp. Start the WhatsApp subscription for the location (a monthly add-on in GoHighLevel, plus small per-message fees from Meta).
2. Connect a business phone number to WhatsApp. It must be a number that is not already on a personal WhatsApp app. The Sterling GoHighLevel number can be used.
3. Finish Meta Business verification if GoHighLevel asks (business name Sterling Fence Staining LLC, the EIN, the website).
4. Create one message template called new_lead_alert, category Utility, in English: "New lead for you. Call within 5 minutes. {{1}} · {{2}} · {{3}}. Profile: {{4}}". Submit it. Meta usually approves Utility templates within minutes to a day.
5. Tell Claude when it is approved. The dashboard switches its WhatsApp alerts to that template (WhatsApp only lets a business start a conversation with an approved template; free-form messages only work within 24 hours of the person writing to you).
6. Have Olga and Lily each send any WhatsApp message to the business number once, so the number is saved on their phones.
7. Press "Send a test" for each person on the On call → Team tab.
