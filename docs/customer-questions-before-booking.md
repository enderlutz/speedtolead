# 25 questions customers ask before booking

Sterling Fence Staining. Compiled 2026-09-29 from production data — not from
memory or guesswork:

- **229 questions** asked in text after an estimate went out and before any
  payment (1 June – 29 Sep)
- **1,938 call transcripts** (1 July – 29 Sep)
- **1,217 call analyses**, Facebook and email threads

Counts shown are how often that theme appeared in the 229 texts. Questions
without a count came mainly from calls, where frequency can't be counted the
same way.

## The pattern

**Almost every one of these is answerable before anyone picks up the phone.**
Sides, packages, colour, coats, repairs, financing, own-stain, the neighbour's
side, HOA, duration, overspray, touch-ups. They are currently asked one at a
time, by text, days apart, because neither the proposal nor the scope image
answers them.

Three are not questions about the service at all, and cost sales directly:

- **#19** — the proposal link failing to open, five separate customers
- **#20** — overspray reaching a neighbour's house
- **#23** — customers unable to tell whether the company is real
